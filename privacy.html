Privacy Policy — Limit Out

Last updated: August 31, 2026

Limit Out is a duck-hunting arcade game. This page explains what data the game collects, how it's used, and who it's shared with.

No accounts, no personal information

Limit Out has no sign-up, no login, and no user accounts. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any other personally-identifying information, and there is nowhere in the game to enter it.

Data stored on your device

Your progress — round, score, unlocked gear, and settings — is saved locally on your device only, using standard browser/app local storage. It is never transmitted to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app or clearing site data erases it permanently; we have no copy and cannot recover it.

Anonymous analytics

We use PostHog, a third-party analytics service, to understand things like how many people play, which rounds they reach, and whether they come back. This is limited to:

  • An anonymous, randomly-generated device identifier (not tied to your name or any account)
  • Gameplay events, e.g. a round starting, a round ending, gear purchased
  • Basic technical data such as device/browser type, screen size, and general (city-level) location inferred from IP address

We do not know who you are from this data. PostHog processes it on our behalf under its own privacy policy. Playing offline, or with an ad/tracker blocker, simply means no analytics events are sent — the game works the same either way.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or share data with advertisers.
  • We don't show ads.
  • We don't access your contacts, camera, microphone, or location beyond the coarse, IP-based location described above.
  • We don't use the data collected to identify you personally.

Children's privacy

Limit Out does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Since no accounts or personal information are collected at all, this applies equally to players of any age.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
